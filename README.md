<h1 align="center">⚖️ NMAK - Legal Assistance Platform</h1>

<p align="center">
  A complete legal help platform built using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>📌 Overview</h2>
<p>
NMAK is a web-based legal assistance platform designed to help users quickly connect with professional lawyers based on their legal needs.  
It also includes emergency features for urgent legal situations, making legal support more accessible and efficient.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>🚀 Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>🔍 Browse lawyers by specialization (Family, Criminal, Corporate, Real Estate)</li>
  <li>📞 Direct contact with lawyers</li>
  <li>📝 User registration and login system</li>
  <li>⚡ Emergency legal assistance feature</li>
  <li>🤖 Basic chatbot support</li>
  <li>📂 Separate portals for users and lawyers</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>🛠️ Tech Stack</h2>
<ul>
  <li><b>Frontend:</b> HTML, CSS, JavaScript</li>
  <li><b>Backend:</b> PHP</li>
  <li><b>Database:</b> MySQL</li>
</ul>

<h2>⚙️ How to Run</h2>
<ol>
  <li>Download or clone the repository</li>
  <li>Move project folder to <b>htdocs</b> (XAMPP) or <b>www</b> (WAMP)</li>
  <li>Start Apache and MySQL</li>
  <li>Open browser and go to:<br>
    <code>http://localhost/NMAK</code>
  </li>
</ol>
